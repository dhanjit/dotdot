How to Play DotDot

Objective

Complete more squares than your opponent to win!

How to Play

  1. Take Turns - Click on any empty line between dots to draw it
  2. Complete Squares - When you draw the 4th side of a square, you capture it!
  3. Extra Turn - Completing a square earns you another turn immediately
  4. Chain Captures - Keep going as long as you complete squares

Winning

The player with the most captured squares when the grid is full wins!

AI Difficulty

  • Greedy - Takes available squares, avoids giving them away
  • Strategic - Advanced tactics, minimizes sacrifices

Visual Aids

  • Red line - Shows the last move made
  • Turn indicator - Shows whose turn it is
  • Scores - Track squares captured by each player
